Status: public preview. Drafts below are prepared for a human to review and post; no social announcements have been sent.

## Positioning

- Name: **Bar To Do**
- Stylized branding: **bar.to.do** (not a domain we own)
- Tagline: **Microsoft To Do, right in your menu bar.**
- Audience: Mac users who already use Microsoft To Do and want quicker access.
- Price: free; MIT-licensed source.
- Website: https://github.com/milo-dotcom/bar-to-do
- Download: https://github.com/milo-dotcom/bar-to-do/releases/tag/v1.2.0

## First promotion: invite testers

Start with 5–10 people who already use To Do on a Mac. Ask whether they can install, sign in, add a disposable task, complete it, and reopen the app after a restart. Ask again after a week whether they still use it. Record issues on GitHub.

The current ZIP is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized, so GitHub/source-oriented testers are the best initial audience. Finish Developer ID signing and notarization before a broad nontechnical launch. Do not ask people to disable macOS security protections.

## Mac community draft

Title: I made a free, open-source Microsoft To Do menu bar companion

I use Microsoft To Do on a Mac and wanted quicker access to capture small tasks. I built Bar To Do with Codex and released the source under MIT.

It's a compact WebKit panel showing Microsoft's official To Do website. You can use its task lists, add and complete tasks, and pin the panel open. It uses normal Microsoft sign-in; it isn't a native WidgetKit widget or a Microsoft product.

This is an early Apple silicon preview. The download is ad-hoc signed and not notarized yet, and organization policies can affect sign-in. I'm looking for testers and feedback rather than claiming it's finished.

Source and download: https://github.com/milo-dotcom/bar-to-do
